02 — Background

A Few Facts

Based in San Luis Obispo, California — Basile Inman doesn't fit in a single column. He holds a B.A. in Media Studies & Computer Science from the University of San Francisco, and has spent the better part of a decade building trails, managing fire crews, and earning City Employee of the Year as a Ranger Foreman for the City of SLO.

When he's not managing open spaces or running a chainsaw, he's designing visuals, building something in the shop, producing music, or shredding trails on a mountain bike. The thread running through all of it: making things with intention, whether that's a trail through steep terrain, a clear infographic, or a well-timed drop.
